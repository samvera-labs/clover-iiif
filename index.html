Why Clover

Reads IIIF Presentation 2, 3 and 4, from the parts up.

Read the docs →

Accessible

WAI-ARIA design patterns

Keyboard navigation, focus and motion preferences in every part.

Composable

Templates, or parts

Take the Viewer in a template, or build a layout of your own from the parts the templates are made of.

Yours

Inherits your page

Clover sets no font and steps every space from your 1rem. Colours are CSS custom properties: set one on any ancestor.

Light

Ships what you draw

The Viewer is a 1 kB shell, so a page carries only the template and parts it uses. Its renderer replaces OpenSeadragon.

Portable

React, or a script tag

ES modules for React 18 and 19, and a <clover-viewer> element for any page.
